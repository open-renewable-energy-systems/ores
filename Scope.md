# Scope

The Open Renewable Energy Systems (ORES) Working Group develops an open standard
architecture, APIs, and communication protocols for renewable energy systems,
initially for residential use cases. The specifications cover system
architecture, the interfaces and protocols among system modules and components
(such as solar generation, storage, inverters, and controllers), and cloud APIs,
so that device manufacturers, hardware and software developers, and DIYers can
implement disaggregated, interoperable, plug-and-play renewable energy
solutions.

The Working Group may also produce reference implementations, guidelines for
utility integration and adoption, and related documentation. The scope may
expand to commercial and industrial usage scenarios in the future.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
